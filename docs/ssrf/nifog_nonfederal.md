## Authorization Requirements

### public_safety_part90
- **Authority:** FCC
- **Class:** Licensed
- **Notes:** Nationwide non-federal public safety interoperability channels as published in the CISA
National Interoperability Field Operations Guide (NIFOG) 2.02. Nationwide VHF/UHF
interoperability channels are designated under 47 CFR 90.20(d)(80); authorized emission for
those channels is 11K0F3E (2.5 kHz deviation narrowband analog FM).

TRANSMIT REQUIRES A PART 90 LICENSE OR OPERATION UNDER A LICENSEE'S AUTHORITY. Amateur and
GMRS operators hold no transmit privileges here. Treat every channel in this plan as
receive-only unless the operator is covered by an appropriate Part 90 authorization.

CTCSS tones are part of the national channel definition and are carried as structured
`mode` data: 156.7 Hz plan-wide, with per-channel overrides where NIFOG differs. Default
monitoring practice is still carrier squelch receive.

VTAC33-38 mobile receive frequencies intentionally duplicate the VTAC11-14 simplex
frequencies; they differ only in mobile transmit frequency and in mobile TX tone (136.5 Hz
vs 156.7 Hz). For receive-only monitoring they add no new frequencies.

VFIRE/VMED/VLAW/VSAR mutual aid channels are recorded at 12.5 kHz per the post-narrowbanding
Part 90 requirement; some legacy systems are still encountered operating wideband.
